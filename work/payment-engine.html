Work / Distributed Systems

National-Scale SIP Payment Engine

Architected a high-concurrency SIP processor using Java, Spring Boot and Zookeeper-based distributed locking to bypass horizontal scaling limitations.

The problem

A recurring-payment workload had to process at national scale while preserving correctness under concurrency. The architecture needed to coordinate work across horizontally scaled workers without allowing duplicate processing.

The approach

The scheduler was designed around distributed coordination, concurrent processing and controlled work distribution. Zookeeper-based distributed locking provided coordination while the application layer handled the payment-processing lifecycle.

The outcome

The resulting architecture reduced processing time from days to hours while creating a foundation for horizontal scaling.

The interesting part was not simply making the scheduler faster; it was making scale, coordination and correctness coexist.
